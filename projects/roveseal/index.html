Good to know

Questions, answered.

Does compression reduce the bag's weight?

No. Compression reduces the volume of certain soft contents by removing excess air. It does not reduce the weight of those items.

Does the backpack include a pump?

The supplier offers a black-with-pump option, but the exact contents of the selected SKU need final confirmation. We will state the included accessories clearly before checkout becomes available.

Will it fit under every airline seat?

Airline personal-item and carry-on rules vary. Final bag measurements are still being verified, so no universal airline fit is claimed.
